# Project and Supply Costing

## الحالة

**Planned architecture; no cost engine or financial data exists.**

Dimensions: company, contract, contract type, project, WBS, cost center, cost code, BOQ/supply BOQ item, supply lot, supplier/subcontractor, source, and date.

Cost types include material, labor, equipment, subcontract, service, transport, mobilization, demobilization, site overhead, company overhead, warranty, and other controlled categories. Supply landed cost may include purchase, freight, insurance, customs, handling, inspection, returns, and allocated overhead.

Measures stay distinct: original/current budget, approved variation, committed cost, actual cost, ETC/EAC, forecast, certified/invoiced revenue, collected cash, expected profit/margin, and cash position. Profitability is not cash flow. Allocation records must state basis (direct, quantity, value, weight, or controlled manual) and source.
