# Project and Contract Workflow

## الحالة

**Partial:** optional project master only. **Planned:** all contracts and operational documents.

## Contract families

Industrial Contracting، Supply Only، Supply & Installation، Maintenance، Service Contract. Each contract shares company/client/project/currency/value/variation dimensions but applies its own quantity, delivery, acceptance, service, and invoicing policy.

## Planned flow

Tender/offer → client obligation → contract → project → WBS/BOQ → budget/baseline → schedule or supply plan → procurement commitments → material/labor/equipment/subcontract/service actuals → delivery/work measurement → acceptance/IPC/invoice → collection → forecast/profitability/cash → closeout.

Supply & Installation must not merge supplied, accepted, installed, certified, and invoiced quantities. Every cost retains source-document, project, WBS, cost center, cost code, BOQ, contract, and date where applicable.

## Change and closeout

Budget revision and change control record cost/time impacts before updating current budget or contract value. Closeout covers practical completion, snag/punch list, final handover, DLP, final account, retention release, guarantee release, and project close. These are planned only.
