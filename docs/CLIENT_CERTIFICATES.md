# Client Certificates, Invoices and Acceptance

## الحالة

**Implemented core:** client invoice/IPC value, retention, advance recovery, VAT, net receivable, GL posting, partial/full collection, and paid status. Measurement/acceptance eligibility, detailed BOQ certification, credit notes, and retention release remain planned.

Contract/BOQ → measured work or accepted supply → delivery/inspection evidence → IPC or invoice → receivable, VAT, retention and advance recovery → collection → retention release.

For Supply Only, invoice eligibility follows delivery/acceptance/milestone terms. For Supply & Installation, delivered, accepted, installed, certified, and invoiced quantities remain independent. A Delivery Note is evidence of delivery, not automatically acceptance, revenue, invoice, or cash.

Certified revenue, invoiced receivable, retention, collected cash, recognized revenue, and remaining contractual value are reported separately according to company policy.
